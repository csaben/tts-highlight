Select text → right-click → Read aloud, or press Alt+Shift+R. Pick where to read (right-click the page, or Alt+Shift+B) shows the readable blocks; click one to start. Holding Alt shows the same preview. Alt+Shift+P pauses, Alt+Shift+X stops. Change keys at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
